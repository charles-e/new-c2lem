Isabel and Ruth are 20-something year-old sisters who love/hate each other, but Isabel is a doll, a literal porcelain doll, like a fancy "American Girl" doll who can talk and moves things with telekinesis.  But early on in the book Isabel has an accident leaving her with a big crack and missing an eye.  Ruth takes it as a quest to get Isabel fixed eventually returning home to ask Dad if he knows anything about where Isabel came from.  Turns out Isabel was made by a witch in France near Marseilles and she is called, "La Frabricante de Poupées" aka "the Dollmaker".  The rest of the story is about meeting the dollmaker/mom to see about fixing Isabel.  But what Isabel really wants is to end up like Ripley from Aliens when she's in the power loader.

This is a funny, horrific novella that reads quickly enough you don't need to worry about pesky reality that might spoil things.
