Sally Diamond's elderly father is dying. In the casual english way, he tells his daughter that after he passes, "to put him in the bin."  And of course Sally being vaguely autistic takes him literally and instead of the bin, since they in fact incinerate their garbage, to the incinerator he goes.  That is the beginning of the story on the page.  The back story which we learn is that Sally was originally Mary and her mother were held for 15 years by a very clever pedophile who flees abroad to successfully evade capture.
