Going back through my recent reads I really enjoyed [When We Were Real](/src/books/When%20We%20Were%20Real/) so I looked for another novel by Daryl Gregory... and here we are.  It took a couple chapters to get in to this book because of needing to understand the context and, additionally, several of the characters are con-man-adjacent hence not obviously sympathetic but now I have a head of steam and this novel has a solid core of sadness. Additionally this story is set in the nineties, with references to Wu-Tang clan, ubiquitous AOL discs as well as Gateway Computers with their holstein themed packaging; which is for me, nostalgic but I wonder how it will age. The voice acting is good, in particular I enjoy the voice of Mitzi, the aged, pack-a-day smoker loanshark.
