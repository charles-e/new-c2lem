* <span meta="1.0@2026-10-18T05:58:14.876Z"></span> You don’t remember why you are there, don’t remember traveling to this house. You do not perseverate on such thoughts, though, which are distant, nagging annoyances, and not as pressing a matter as the in-media-res conversation and your hummingbird-wings eyelids. You mash your palms into your eyes again. Your hands sink inside your head, to the depth of your wrists, mercifully stopping the manic, strobing flutter. Still, somehow, you are able to see. You are marooned on a pea-green couch, a long one that extends to the edges of your periphery. You sit slouched and your legs are vines growing haphazardly into the room.
