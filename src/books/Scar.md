Finally got around to reading this tome which is now not quite 25 years old.  This is the second book in the "Bas-Lag" trilogy that made Mieville's rep. The books demonstrate amazing steam-punk world building that notably features "remade men", who have undergone magico-surgical procedures to replace their heads, limbs or bodies with animal or mechanical substitutes. Being remade and enslaved is, in general, reserved as a drastic punishment for criminals.  Besides the remade there are multiple sentient races and not one gods damned elf or hobbit, instead, to pick one, the khepri who have a human body and the head of a beetle.   

The book primarily follows the journey of Bellis Coldwine a 30-something political dissident and academic translator from New Crobuzon who starts the immigration to a safer colony when her ship is captured by pirates from the city of Armada and from then on follows her as a citizen of Armada.  Armada is, as the name suggests, a migrating, peregrinating city that is an composed of repurposed boats lashed together, whose livelihood is trade and privateering.

Bellis, with her translator's skill set, becomes a peripheral participant, well-place narrator, in the Armada's complicated, controversial scheme to visit "The Scar", a legendary discontinuity (effectively a tear) in the seemingly flat world of Bas Lag. What will it mean to sail over the edge of the Scar? Along the way there is much betrayal, intrigue and couple very serious battle involving the Armada.  

In addition to the many interesting fantastic devices that absolutely retain the reader's attention, the character of Bellis is also intriguing.  I wanted to like her, but she is not absolutely likable, particularly because she is self-centered and always thinks that she is the smartest person in the room.  She manages to have a sex-life but she doesn't have any friends. She gravitates toward other important characters as friends and each case she winds up confessing that in retrospect she was used, manipulated to do other's bidding with bad personal consequences.
