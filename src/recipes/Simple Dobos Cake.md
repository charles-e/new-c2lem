## Notes:
* We had this for my birthday and let my girlfriend make it (which is a test in and of itself.)  

* Much fancier versions of this cake (more "Torte" less cake) have six layers with pastry cream filling but this one works because the frosting is apparently 80% of the differentiator.

* This was a pre-inflationary shrinkage recipe so now the cake mix is smaller but to make up for it, sooo is the pudding mix -- anyway the recipe still works.


### Equipment
* 13x9 cake pan
* a mixer (maybe - bowl, spoon and arm will also work)
* sauce pan

## Ingredients
#### Cake
* 1 pkg (18.5 oz) devil's food cake mix
* 1 pkg (3 3/4 oz) instant chocolate pudding
* 1 can (12 oz) lemon lime soda
* 1/2 cup salad oil
* 3 eggs

#### Frosting
* 1 cup sugar
* 2 cups water
* 1/4 cup butter or margarine
* 1/4 teaspoon salt
* 1/3 cup cornstarch
* 1/2 cup sweetened cocoa powder

## Directions
Preheat oven to 350F.

#### Cake
* Combine all the cake ingredients.
* Mix it up thoroughly.
* Pour in to cake pan.
* Bake 30 to 35 minutes .
* Start the frosting while baking.
* Allow cake to cool then frost.

#### Frosting 

* In a saucepan, combine sugar, 1 1/2 cups of the water, butter, and salt; bring to a boil.
* In a small bowl, combine the cornstarch, the remaining 1/2 cup water, and cocoa.
* Stir until smooth; add to hot mixture.
* Cook on medium heat, stirring constantly, until smooth and thick.
* Remove from heat and continue stirring until cool.
